## Brand & Style

The design system projects momentum, meticulous preparation, and focused ambition. Engineered for ambitious professionals navigating high-stakes career transitions, the interface operates with the precision of an executive cockpit and the clarity of a boutique editorial publication. The emotional tone must evoke calm mastery under pressure, reducing the ambient cognitive load of job hunts into structured, actionable progress.

The aesthetic fuses **Tactile Modernism** with **High-Craft Minimalism**:
- Crisp, feather-weight borders delineate structured data panels without heavy visual noise.
- Translucent tonal surfaces with micro-blur allow background metrics and deep kanban boards to feel dimensional yet grounded.
- Focused semantic color coding turns chaotic application pipelines into instantaneous visual signals.
- Intentional density ensures critical interview intelligence, timelines, and talking points remain immediately scannable.

## Colors

The palette relies on a deep slate/zinc midnight foundation (`#090D16` to `#1E293B`) paired with high-resonance status pigments to communicate stage progression instantly:

- **Primary (`#4F46E5` - Deep Indigo):** Represents action, focus, and core platform mechanics. Used for primary CTAs, active pipeline stages, active tab accents, and focus indicators.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` - Refined Emerald):** Dedicated exclusively to victory milestones—offers received, accepted stages, target salary matches, and successful prep milestones.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Kinetic Amber):** Signals urgency and high-engagement phases—scheduled interviews, live recruiter follow-ups, and upcoming countdowns.
- **Accent - Applied (`#0EA5E9` - Sky):** Indicates active outgoing applications, submitted assignments, and waiting states.
- **Neutral Palette:** Ranges from absolute ink backgrounds (`#090D16`) to subtle surface containers (`#0F172A`, `#1E293B`), complemented by crisp, high-legibility text tiers ranging from pure white (`#F8FAFC`) to muted slate metadata (`#64748B`).

## Typography

The typography pairing combines the geometry and executive presence of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headers with the legibility of **Inter** for data tables, form controls, and editorial interview notes.

- **Display & Headlines:** Set tight tracking (`-0.025em` to `-0.015em`) on Plus Jakarta Sans to prevent large headings from sprawling across compact mobile cards.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Quantitative interview data (salaries, rounds completed, dates) should leverage tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) in Inter to align seamlessly in dense list views.
- **Pill Badges & Stage Chips:** Set in `label-sm` or `label-md` using uppercase or title case with deliberate tracking (`0.02em` - `0.04em`) to maintain sharp legibility against colored backdrops.

## Layout & Spacing

The system implements a fluid, responsive 12-column layout grid on desktop and a high-density, horizontal-snapping columnar system on mobile devices.

- **Mobile Viewport (<768px):** Uses `margin: 1rem` and compact `space-sm` / `space-md` gaps. Kanban boards transform into edge-to-edge horizontally swipeable stage lanes or segmented controls to maximize card width.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** Uses `margin: 1.5rem` with a hybrid split view (application list on left, interview prep dossier on right).
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Scales to 12 columns with maximum container constraints of `1440px` and `margin: 2.5rem`. The kanban board displays all major lifecycle columns simultaneously (Saved, Applied, Interviewing, Offer).
- **Internal Spacing:** Micro-paddings inside job cards leverage `space-sm` (vertical) and `space-md` (horizontal) to preserve vertical screen estate without creating visual crowding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through **structural tonal layering** combined with **subtle tinted backdrops and hairline borders**, rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Canvas Base (Level 0):** Pure dark background (`#090D16`), completely neutral and non-reflective.
- **Surface Panels & Lanes (Level 1):** Subtly raised dark slate container (`#0F172A`) bordered by a `1px` translucent boundary (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
- **Interactive Cards (Level 2):** Elevated surface (`#1E293B`) framed with a crisp boundary (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`). On hover or touch selection, cards emit a faint, localized ambient glow tinted with the card’s status color (e.g., Indigo for applied, Amber for interview).
- **Floating Overlays & Bottom Nav (Level 3):** Frosted glass surfaces (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.85)`) with an `18px` background blur, anchored by a hairline top border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined balance between technical rigor and modern warmth:

- **Standard Containers & Cards:** Utilize `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem / 8px) for job cards, input modules, and stage containers, conveying an organized, modular appearance.
- **Modals, Flyouts & Action Sheets:** Use `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) or `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) for top-sheet corners on mobile to feel tactile and native.
- **Pill Badges & Stage Indicator Chips:** Pure pill shapes (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for pipeline stage flags (e.g., "Interviewing", "Offer Extended"), ensuring instant differentiation from structural layout cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled with `#4F46E5`, crisp white text, `label-md` font weight, subtle inset highlight along top edge. Hover states increase brightness slightly.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent surface with a `1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)` and text color `#F8FAFC`. Active state tints the background to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- **Destructive:** Desaturated wine container with high-contrast crimson text and border.

### Status Badges & Chips
- Designed as micro-pills (`rounded-full`, height 24px, padding 0 10px).
- **Offer:** Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, text `#10B981`, border `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`.
- **Interviewing:** Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`, text `#F59E0B`, border `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`.
- **Applied:** Background `rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.12)`, text `#0EA5E9`, border `rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.3)`.
- **Saved / Prospect:** Background `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.12)`, text `#94A3B8`, border `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.25)`.

### Kanban Job Cards
- Compact composite card (`#1E293B`) with `1px` edge borders.
- Top row: Company avatar icon (24px) alongside job title, flanked by status badge.
- Middle: Salary range, location type (Remote/Hybrid), and stage indicator.
- Footer: Next action countdown (e.g., "Prep Session - Tomorrow, 2:00 PM") with a mini-progress pip for interview rounds (e.g., "Round 3 of 4").

### Form Inputs & Interview Notes
- Dark recessed background (`#0B0F19`) surrounded by `1px` border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).
- On focus, border transitions to `#4F46E5` accompanied by an indigo micro-ring (`0 0 0 1px #4F46E5`).

### Sleek Bottom Navigation
- Anchored at bottom with safe-area insets.
- Height: 64px, backed by dark translucent glass (`rgba(9, 13, 22, 0.85)` + blur).
- Active item displays an indigo micro-indicator line at the top and an intensified icon fill.

### Interview Prep Dossier Card
- Dedicated AI-coaching widget featuring an accent border along the left spine (`3px solid #F59E0B` for live rounds).
- Provides collapsible accordion cards for STAR-method question breakdowns, company intelligence cheat-sheets, and counter-question templates.
